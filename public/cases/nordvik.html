Gallery I · Context
A furniture brand, standing
on new ground.

Nordvik's promise has always been made to be lived on. The floor is the one surface it has never touched, and where home textiles are growing fastest in Europe.

A rug, in the room it was made for

"Launches rarely fail on the offering. They fail when the story isn't aligned before launch day."

A perspective carried from four and a half years inside EY, where the same pattern showed up in every CRM rollout that struggled: the product was rarely the problem.

The real risk isn't a slow first collection. It's a weak one, which makes the next attempt harder, not just this one.

Gallery II · The Customer
Not everyone. Someone first.

"Rug shoppers" is too broad an audience for a launch. The highest-converting, lowest-cost audience is already on Nordvik's own list.

A rug, already living with someone
The Repeat Nordvik Buyer
Who she is
Owns: two or more Nordvik pieces already
Spend: €800+ lifetime value
Behaviour: opens every email, rarely buys on discount
Why she converts first
Already trusts Nordvik's durability claims
Needs "why now," not "why textiles"
Becomes the referral engine for everyone after her
Key insight
Launch to the believers before the browsers. Most brand extensions stumble by chasing new customers before proving the idea to their own.
Gallery III · The Position
Made to be lived on,
made to be loud.

Nordvik's ethic has always been quiet confidence. This collection extends it somewhere the brand has never gone: colour, and pattern.

Saturated colour, no apology
Collection positioning statement
For Nordvik customers who've kept their home quiet everywhere else, Bold Underfoot is the one surface designed to be loud, made with the same durability promise as everything else Nordvik sells.
For whom
Customers who trust Nordvik but play it safe on colour
What it is
Permission to be bold, backed by a brand already trusted
Key benefit
Expression, without the risk of an unknown brand
Against whom
Story-less mass DTC, inaccessible artisan rugs

"Made to be lived on" becomes "made to be lived in loudly."

Same durability standard. New emotional register.

Not a trend collabNot a discount lineNot a rebrand

Gallery VII · What's Next
Success is its own risk.

The next ninety days matter as much as the first. Over-extending the capsule too fast dilutes exactly what made it credible.

One more colourway, pointed at what's next
I
Earn the second drop
Only expand past three SKUs once sell-through and NPS both clear target.
II
Trade show presence
Apply for a stand at one regional design fair once wholesale hits double digits.
III
Materials story
Document wool sourcing now, before it's needed for a larger sustainability claim.
IV
A colourway, not a collection
One new colourway each season, inside the same three silhouettes, protecting the evergreen line.
