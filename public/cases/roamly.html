Sec. 01 · Market Landscape

Is there room for a new voice here?

Europe has 180,000+ independent hotels: characterful, underserved, and stuck choosing between costly enterprise CRMs, generic SaaS, or OTAs that own the guest relationship. Roamly's problem was never the product. It's that nobody agrees on what to call it.

Personal perspective, EY background

"At EY, I saw CRM implementations fail not because the product was wrong, but because the value proposition was never made clear to the end user. Roamly's problem is identical: a genuinely useful product losing ground because the message doesn't reach the person who needs it."

The core brand problem

Every salesperson describes Roamly differently: CRM, booking tool, guest experience platform. No consistent story, no clear reason to choose it.

Sec. 02 · Customer Definition

Who exactly is this for?

"Independent boutique hotels" is a category, not a person. Using Jobs-to-be-Done, I built one vivid ICP instead: the highest-value, highest-conversion segment.

Ideal Customer Profile · Primary PersonaSofia, the independent hotel owner-operator
Who she is
Role: Owner-GM, 18–35 rooms
Team: 3–8 staff, wears every hat
Revenue: €400k–€1.2M / yr
Her frustrations
Pays 20% commission for guests she already knew
No scalable way to follow up personally
Tried HubSpot. Three weeks in, gave up.
Jobs to be done
Win more direct bookings over OTAs
Remember guest preferences at scale
Feel modern, not just surviving
she's the reason none of this can be generic
Key insight

Sofia doesn't want a CRM. She wants her guests to feel like they're coming home. The tech is the enabler; the feeling is the product.

Sec. 03 · Brand Positioning

What does Roamly stand for?

Positioning is the one decision everything else (messaging, design, channels, pricing) flows from.

Roamly · Brand Positioning Statement
For independent boutique hotel owners who believe hospitality is personal, Roamly is the guest relationship platform that makes every return guest feel like the hotel never forgot them, unlike CRMs or OTAs that treat guests as transactions.
For whom
Owners who compete on character, not scale
What we are
The guest relationship platform for independent hospitality
Key benefit
Every guest feels remembered, with no manual effort
Against whom
CRMs and OTAs treating guests as bookings

Brand territory

Roamly sits between warmth and precision: a family-run hotel's memory, made scalable.

Brand character, three words

Warm. Precise. Yours.

What Roamly is not

Equally important: what it should never be mistaken for.

Not a CRMNot enterpriseNot a booking engine

Sec. 07 · What I'd Do Next
