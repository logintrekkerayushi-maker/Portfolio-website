# The Curious Atlas — Art brief

Style for every image: painterly anime landscape in the spirit of Studio Ghibli and
Frieren backgrounds. Soft painted brushwork, enormous cumulus clouds, warm sunlight,
lush greens, no characters, no text, no watermark, no logo. Same world, same light,
same palette as the concept board: forest green #1B4D3E, sage #6FAF7F, sky #7EC8F8,
cloud white #F1F6FF, coral #FF8A7A accents in flowers.

Export: PNG or high-quality JPG/WebP. Landscapes 2560×1440 unless noted.

## 1. hero-landscape — 2560×1440 (replaces the board crop)
A vast green valley seen from a flowering hilltop meadow. A worn stone path winds
down toward a lake with a small castle town on its far shore. Distant blue mountains.
Gigantic painted clouds, blue sky. A wooden signpost on the left, a big tree trunk at
the far left with leafy branches hanging into the top-left corner. Morning light.
Keep the top 45% mostly sky so text can sit on the left.

## 2. library-exterior — 2560×1440
A castle-like library with pale stone towers and tall arched windows, glimpsed through
summer trees from a forest path. Dappled light, late morning.

## 3. library-interior — 2560×1440
Inside a grand sunlit library: tall wooden shelves full of books, three arched windows
pouring light, climbing plants, a long reading table, floating dust. Golden afternoon.
Keep the centre-bottom third fairly calm (no busy detail) so a bookshelf can sit there.

## 4. expedition-map — 2048×1365 (landscape, 3:2)
An old parchment expedition map lying on a mossy stone in a sunlit clearing, seen from
slightly above. Painted ON the map, as a tiny dimensional world: three places along a
winding trail — (a) a small harbour town with a lighthouse at the lower left,
(b) a walled market town with a clock tower in the centre, (c) a mountain camp with
tents and a lantern at the upper right. A compass rose, a lake, little forests and
hills, the trail drawn as a dotted line joining the three places. Beside the map on
the stone: a brass compass, a magnifying glass, a leather notebook. Warm light.
No text or labels on the map (the site adds the names).

## 5. strategy-clearing — 2560×1440
A sunlit forest clearing with a shallow stream running between mossy banks and
wildflowers, shafts of light through tall trees. Midday.

## 6. studio-exterior — 2560×1440
A cosy wooden workshop cottage nestled among trees, warm light in its windows,
a chimney with a thread of smoke, flowers by the door. Late afternoon.

## 7. studio-interior — 2560×1440
Inside the creative workshop: a big window onto green trees, a wooden desk with
sketchbooks, paint jars and brushes, pinned sketches and a corkboard on the wall,
hanging plants, a lantern. Warm golden light. Keep the corkboard area clear so
posters can be pinned on it by the site.

## 8. viewpoint-sunset — 2560×1440
A stone viewpoint with a low wall and a wooden bench, looking over the lake valley
toward the mountains at sunset. Orange and violet sky, first stars appearing, warm
haze over the water.

## 9. cloud sprites — 4 images, 1600×800 each
Single painted cumulus clouds on a flat pure blue (#2E8BE8) background, no gradient,
so they can be cut out. One tall, one wide, two small.

## 10. canopy — 1600×700
Leafy tree branches hanging from the top-left corner on a flat pure blue background,
same style as the hero.
